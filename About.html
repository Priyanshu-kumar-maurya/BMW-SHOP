Corporate Profile

Pioneering the Ultimate Driving Machine

Bayerische Motoren Werke AG (BMW) is a globally renowned German multinational manufacturer of luxury automobiles and motorcycles headquartered in Munich, Bavaria, Germany.

Under its four legendary brands — BMW, MINI, Rolls-Royce, and BMW Motorrad — the BMW Group delivers premium customer experiences across more than 140 countries. In 2023 alone, the company produced over 2,555,000 vehicles worldwide and consistently ranks among the top global leaders in innovation and sustainability.

With manufacturing facilities across Germany, the United States, South Africa, the United Kingdom, Mexico, India, and China, BMW unites precision craftsmanship with groundbreaking technological visionary spirit.

Our History

Milestones of Excellence

A journey through over a century of bold reinvention and unmatched engineering leadership.

March 7, 1916

Founding of Bayerische Flugzeugwerke (BFW)

BMW traces its roots to aircraft manufacturer Bayerische Flugzeugwerke, established in Munich. In 1917, Rapp Motorenwerke adopted the iconic name Bayerische Motoren Werke (BMW) with the famous blue-and-white propeller roundel.

1923

The Legendary BMW R32 Motorcycle

Designed by Chief Engineer Max Fritz, the BMW R32 featured a horizontally-opposed Boxer engine and shaft drive — a blueprint of mechanical precision that defined BMW Motorrad for the next century.

1959

The "Neue Klasse" Era & Financial Rebirth

Following strategic investment by Herbert and Harald Quandt, BMW introduced the 'Neue Klasse' sports sedans (BMW 1500), cementing the brand's identity for agile handling and driver-focused luxury.

1972 - Present

Birth of BMW M & The Modern Electrified Era

From the founding of BMW Motorsport GmbH (M Power) in 1972 to the launch of BMW i electric vehicles in 2011 and today’s ultra-advanced Neue Klasse electric architectures, BMW continues to redefine the future of sheer driving pleasure.
